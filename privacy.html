Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy covers Wonder Lab Uploader, a private tool the Wonder Lab creator uses to publish Wonder Lab's own videos to Wonder Lab's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account.

YouTube API Services

Wonder Lab Uploader uses YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your information is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the channel of the Google account that signs in (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read, change or delete other videos, comments, subscribers or any other channel data.
  • Permission to publish posts and Reels to the Wonder Lab Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account.

What is stored, and where

The only data stored is the sign-in tokens needed to upload, kept in a local file on the creator's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server, and no data is collected from viewers or any other people.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties. The tool sends videos and their titles, descriptions and tags only to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram, to publish them.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings page, and from Facebook under Settings → Business integrations. Deleting the local token file also removes the tool's access.
